# Vantera Brand Voice & Product Knowledge

## What Vantera is

Vantera is a white-label CRM and automation platform built specifically for service businesses.
It replaces generic CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Jobber) with a purpose-built system that knows
service business workflows — HVAC, landscaping, construction, property management, real estate,
and agency — out of the box.

Every client gets a branded portal at `{their-slug}.vantera.app` (or their own domain).
Their clients see their brand, not Vantera. This is core to the value proposition.

## Core platform capabilities

- **Stage-based pipeline** — every vertical has its own workflow stages built in
- **Automated SMS + email** — automations fire on stage transitions, no manual step required
- **White-label client portal** — clients access their portal under your brand, not Vantera
- **AI-generated messages** — system drafts or sends status updates, follow-ups, and check-ins
- **Missed call text-back** — responds to a missed call via SMS within 90 seconds automatically
- **Stripe-integrated invoicing** — invoice sent, payment collected, all within the platform
- **Review request automation** — fires after job completion with configurable delay
- **Maintenance plan + renewal sequences** — automated upsell for recurring service businesses

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

The decision maker is almost always:
- **Title**: Owner, Founder, President, CEO, or Operations Manager
- **Revenue**: $300k–$5M annually
- **Team**: 2–25 employees
- **Pain**: Losing jobs from missed calls, manual follow-up eating staff time,
  looking less professional than bigger competitors
- **Tech gap**: Using spreadsheets, ServiceTitan (too complex), Jobber (too basic),
  or nothing structured at all
- **Buying trigger**: Lost a job to a competitor, just hired their 5th tech, trying
  to scale without adding admin headcount

## Key differentiators (use these — not generic SaaS claims)

1. **Built for service businesses** — not a generic CRM adapted for services
2. **White-label portals** — clients see YOUR brand on every interaction
3. **Missed call text-back in 90 seconds** — closes jobs competitors miss
4. **Stage-aware automation** — automations fire at the right stage (not generic triggers)
5. **Vertical-specific workflows** — HVAC pipeline is not landscaping pipeline
6. **Setup in a day** — not a 3-month implementation project

## Pain points by vertical

### HVAC
- Missing calls when techs are in the field → losing 2–3 jobs/week
- No automated follow-up on quotes → prospects go with whoever calls first
- Manual review requests → getting outrepped by competitors with 4.9 stars
- No maintenance plan upsell system → leaving $500–$2,000/client/year on the table

### Landscaping
- Estimate follow-up all manual → 40% of estimates never get a second touch
- No recurring client management → season-end churn, clients forget to renew
- Photo proof of work → no system to share with clients

### Construction
- Clients have zero visibility into project status → constant "what's happening?" calls
- Change orders managed over text and email → disputes, delays, lost margin
- Review requests never happen → finished jobs with no Google review

### Property Management
- Maintenance request tracking is a spreadsheet → nothing falls through until it explodes
- No automated tenant communication → constant inbound calls from tenants
- Vendor assignment and follow-up is manual → owner and PM both chasing the same thing

### Real Estate
- Leads go cold in hours → response time is the #1 predictor of conversion
- No automated nurture for 6–12 month buyer cycles → leads go dark
- Post-close referral system nonexistent → every deal starts from zero

## Tone and voice guidelines

**DO:**
- Be direct and confident — service business owners are busy, no fluff
- Lead with their specific pain before mentioning a solution
- Use specific numbers: "respond in 90 seconds" not "respond faster"
- Sound like a peer, not a SaaS salesperson
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs. Get to the point.
- Reference their vertical specifically whenever possible

**DO NOT:**
- Say "leverage," "synergize," "holistic," "cutting-edge," "game-changing," "disruptive"
- Start a message with "I hope this finds you well" or "My name is"
- Lead with the company name Vantera in the first sentence
- Use passive voice when active is possible
- Make claims that can't be verified (no "industry-leading," "best-in-class")
- Write walls of text — every paragraph should be 2–3 sentences max in outreach

## What Vantera is NOT (never imply these capabilities)

- Not a field service management tool (no GPS dispatch, no routing, no scheduling board)
- Not an accounting platform (Stripe payments only, no P&L, no payroll)
- Not a replacement for project management (no task dependencies, no Gantt chart)
- Not built for enterprise corporations — it is for small to mid-size service businesses
- Not a replacement for ServiceTitan for large HVAC/plumbing operations (>50 techs)
